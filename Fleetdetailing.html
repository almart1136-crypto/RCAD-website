Standard Fleet Detail

What Every Vehicle Gets

This is our baseline fleet detail, designed to keep work vehicles clean and presentable without pulling them off the road longer than necessary. Contracts can add paint correction, graphics care, or full VIP details on executive vehicles.

Interior

  • Vacuum carpets, seats, and cargo areas
  • Wipe down dash, console, door panels, and hard surfaces
  • Clean interior glass and mirrors
  • Spot-treat stains as needed
  • Light odor treatment

Exterior

  • Complete hand wash
  • Clean wheels, tires, and wheel wells
  • Bug and tar removal on front-facing surfaces
  • Wax or sealant protection
  • Dress tires and trim
  • Clean exterior glass and mirrors

Optional Add-Ons

  • Cab and cargo-area deep clean
  • Vinyl wrap and graphic care (gentle hand wash, no abrasives)
  • Paint correction for resale or trade-in units
  • Odor treatment for smoke or heavy-use vehicles
