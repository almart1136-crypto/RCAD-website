Full Reset

VIP Detail

The complete inside-and-out package. We work from the headliner down to the carpets and from the roof to the exhaust tips — roughly an eight-hour process that leaves your vehicle looking and feeling refreshed.

What's included

  • Shampoo and hot-water extraction of all carpets, floor mats, and seats
  • Dirt and stain removal from the headliner
  • Every crack, crevice, and panel detailed
  • Windows, mirrors, and sunroof cleaned inside and out
  • Leather seats freshened and rejuvenated
  • Glove box and center console wiped clean inside
  • Spare-tire compartment and trunk vacuumed
  • Complete hand wash with steam rinse
  • Clay-bar treatment for brake dust, rail dust, bugs, and tar
  • Wheels, wheel wells, and rims properly cleaned
  • Door jambs and inside fuel door wiped down
  • Tires, trim, and grille dressed with premium exterior dressing
  • Durable wax sealant applied
  • Exhaust tips cleaned and polished
