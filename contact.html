Common Questions

Before You Contact Us

The answers to the questions we get most often. If yours isn't here, call us — we're happy to talk it through.

How long does a detail take?

It depends on the package. Our VIP Detail takes roughly eight hours. Interior-only details run 2.5 to 5 hours, and exterior-only details take 1 to 2.5 hours. Paint correction adds time based on the condition of your clear coat. We'll give you a time estimate when you book.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — we work by appointment only. We don't take walk-ins because each vehicle gets a dedicated block of time and our full attention. Call 616-485-4306 or use the booking page to schedule.

Do I need to be there during the detail?

No. Customers drop their vehicle off with a driver and return once we notify them it's finished. We'll text or call when it's ready for pickup.

How do I get a quote?

Call 616-485-4306, send a message through the form above, or email alekmartinez@rivercityautodetail.com. For the most accurate quote, send a few photos of your vehicle — interior, exterior, and any problem areas. We'll quote based on vehicle size and condition.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in South Grand Rapids and serve the surrounding neighborhoods, including Burton Heights, Black Hills, Godwin Heights, and the South East End. We also work with dealerships and fleets across the Grand Rapids area. Not sure if we cover your area? Give us a call.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, card, Stripe, and checks. If you're having more than three vehicles detailed within one week, we'll send an invoice due within 7 days.
