Our Story

From Excellence Auto Restore to River City Auto Detail

River City Auto Detail didn't start with a big launch or a marketing plan. It started in 2023 as Excellence Auto Restore Center — a one-person operation run by Luis Torres, who had spent years working inside one of the largest detailing operations in the Midwest before deciding to build something of his own.

In January 2026, Luis partnered with Alek Martinez. Alek came from a sales and marketing background, and together the two saw an opportunity to bring a growth mindset to a shop that was already doing the work well. That partnership became the DBA River City Auto Detail — a new name, the same standard, and a clearer direction.

Today the shop is still just the two of them. That's intentional. When the owners are the ones doing the work, there's no layer between you and the person detailing your vehicle. No rushed jobs, no shortcuts, no passing your car to someone who doesn't care about the result.

Our Focus

Who We Serve

Most of our work comes from B2B partners — dealerships and fleets across Grand Rapids who need volume without sacrificing quality. That's the standard we bring to every retail customer, too. Same work, same care, every vehicle.

Our Standard

High-End Quality, No Excuses

Our customers are people who notice the details — the door jamb, the headliner, the wheel well. We built this shop for them. If the finish isn't right, we fix it. That's not a promise we make lightly; it's the reason we exist.

The Owners

Two People. One Standard.

River City Auto Detail is owned and operated by Luis Torres and Alek Martinez. They do the work themselves — every vehicle, every time.

Co-Owner

Luis Torres

Luis founded the business in 2023 after years of working inside one of the largest detailing operations in the Midwest. He brought that experience — the volume, the process, the standard — to his own shop. His focus is the work itself: the interiors, the paint, the details most people never see.

Co-Owner

Alek Martinez

Alek joined as a partner in January 2026, bringing a background in sales and marketing and a vision for growth. He handles the business side — the dealership relationships, the scheduling, the customers — so Luis can stay focused on the vehicles. Together, they run the shop as a true partnership.

Our Guarantee

If Something's Wrong, We'll Make It Right.

We guarantee the work is done within 24 hours of drop-off, at the quality level you paid for. If you notice something we missed — a missed area of what was included in your package — here's how we handle it:

1

Tell us within 48 hours

Once you notice the issue, let us know. We have 48 hours to re-detail the affected area.

2

We fix it — free

We return to the affected area and correct it at no charge. Most issues are resolved the same day.

3

If it's still not right, we'll make it right

If the correction doesn't meet your standard, we'll work with you on a fair resolution — including a refund when that's the right call.

Our mistake means a missed area of what was included in the package you paid for. That's it — no fine print, no excuses.
